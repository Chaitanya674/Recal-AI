Recal privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Recal is a learning assistant built for the Google AI Builder hackathon. It plans study time, makes short visual explainers and study notes, and rehearses presentations with you. This page explains what data Recal uses and why.

What we collect

  • What you type or upload: chat messages, links and documents (PPTX, DOCX, XLSX) you share, so Recal can answer, build notes and run practice meetings.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): only if you click "Connect Google Calendar". Recal reads your events to find free time for study sessions. It never edits or deletes your Google events.
  • Google Docs: only if you click "Connect Google Docs". Recal can create new documents when you export a card. It can only access files it created itself (the drive.file permission), not the rest of your Drive.
  • Your Google email address, to show which account is connected.

How it is used

Your data is used only to provide Recal's features to you. Text you send is processed by Google's Gemini models on Google Cloud (Vertex AI) to generate answers, notes, plans and narration. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Recal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the project that runs Recal. Google sign-in tokens are encrypted before they are stored.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Calendar or Docs at any time from the app. You can also remove Recal's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The Reset button in the app clears your chat, cards and Recal study sessions.
  • To have all your data deleted, email us and we will remove it.
